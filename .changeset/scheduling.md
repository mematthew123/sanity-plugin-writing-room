Schedule from the room. Where the Studio has Sanity's scheduled drafts, the panel that asks before publishing also offers **Schedule for later**: pick a time and the document goes live by itself, and a document with no published date gets that time as its date (a date the Writer set is left alone). A scheduled document is locked in the room and shows exactly what will go live; **Unlock** takes it off the schedule so the words or the time can be changed. Nothing to configure, and nothing changes in a Studio without scheduled drafts.
