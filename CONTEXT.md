# Writing Room

A calm, full-screen place to write long-form documents inside a Sanity Studio. This is the language the room uses for what a Writer does there and what can stop them.

## Language

**Hold**:
A refusal, placed on a document by one of the Studio's workflows, of publishing, unpublishing or deleting it for as long as the workflow keeps it in a stage. The room knows a workflow only through its holds.
_Avoid_: Guard, lock, block, freeze

**Held**:
What the room calls a document under a **Hold**, in the Writer's words: "A workflow is holding this post." A held document can still be written in.
_Avoid_: Locked, frozen, in review

**Locked**:
A scheduled document, which can't be written in until the Writer unlocks it. Scheduling causes it; a workflow never does.
_Avoid_: Held, read-only
