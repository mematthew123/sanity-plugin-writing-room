# Writing Room

A calm, full-screen place to write in a Sanity Studio. This glossary fixes the words used for the room and for what happens in it.

## Language

### The room

**Writer**:
The person writing in the room.
_Avoid_: User, author, editor

**Prose**:
The title and the body of a document: the view the Writer writes in.
_Avoid_: Content, main view

**Details**:
Every field of a document that isn't Prose, and the view that shows them.
_Avoid_: Metadata, settings, document fields

### Bringing a piece in

**Whole-piece paste**:
A paste into an empty document that carries a complete piece written elsewhere, rather than a passage to work with.
_Avoid_: Import, full-document paste

**Sorting**:
Putting the parts of a whole-piece paste into the fields they belong to. It only places what was pasted or picks among options the document already offers; it never writes.
_Avoid_: Import, content mapping, field mapping, AI fill

**Placing**:
The part of Sorting that moves text from the paste into a field, such as the headline into the title.
_Avoid_: Extraction, mapping

**Picking**:
The part of Sorting that chooses among the answers a field already offers, such as an existing author or category. A pick is stated when the paste names it and judged when it follows from what the piece is about.
_Avoid_: Classification, tagging, inference

**Sorting offer**:
The one-time question the room asks after a whole-piece paste. Sorting happens only if the Writer accepts it.
_Avoid_: Import prompt, import button
